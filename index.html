In every room

Each room is open-plan and self-contained, so you can arrive late, work, cook a little and sleep well.

  • A kitchenette

    Microwave, fridge, kettle, tea and coffee, crockery and cutlery. The Rock Room adds a stove and oven.

  • A desk and free Wi-Fi

    A proper workstation with a lamp, and Wi-Fi that also runs the smart TV.

  • Your own bathroom

    An en-suite shower in three rooms and a bath in the Light Room. Basins sit on concrete tops over face-brick plinths.

  • A door of your own

    Every room has a private entrance onto a patio or courtyard. The Royal and Vision rooms face the Mooi River.

  • Cool rooms, safe parking

    Air conditioning in every room for Highveld summers, and free, secure parking behind the gate.
