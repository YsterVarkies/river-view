Questions

Is breakfast included?

No. River View is self-catering. Every room has a kitchenette with a microwave, a fridge, and tea and coffee, and the Rock Room has a stove and oven.

Is there parking?

Yes. Parking is free and secure, inside the gate on Postma Street.

Is there Wi-Fi?

Yes, free in every room. It also runs the smart TV.

Do the rooms have air conditioning?

Yes, all four do. The Rock Room has a fan as well.

Can I bring children?

All ages are welcome. The Rock Room sleeps three; the other rooms sleep two.

Which room has a bath?

The Light Room. The Royal, Vision and Rock rooms have showers.

How close is the university?

The North-West University campus begins about 120 m from the gate in a straight line, and the NWU Botanical Garden is about 320 m away.

Where can I walk or run?

Along the Mooi River and around the Potchefstroom Dam, whose shore is about 550 m from the gate. The area is popular for walks and runs.
